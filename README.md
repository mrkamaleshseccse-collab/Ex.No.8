# EXP.NO.8 – REPRODUCING AN IMAGE USING PROMPTS FOR IMAGE GENERATION

**Date:** 03-09-2026

**Register Number:** 212225040164

## AIM

To study how AI image-generation tools can recreate a reference image using suitable text prompts. The experiment focuses on observing the important visual elements of an image, creating a simple prompt, improving it with more details, and comparing the generated image with the original to achieve better similarity.

## TOOLS USED

* Stable Diffusion
* Midjourney
* AI-based image generation tools

## PROCEDURE

1. Examine the reference image carefully and identify its main objects and overall appearance.
2. Observe important features such as colors, shapes, textures, lighting, shadows, background, and composition.
3. Write a simple prompt describing the main subject of the image.
4. Improve the prompt by adding details about objects, colors, lighting, mood, position, texture, and camera view.
5. Mention the required visual style such as realistic, cinematic, artistic, CGI, or cartoon.
6. Enter the improved prompt into an AI image-generation tool.
7. Generate the image and compare it with the reference image.
8. Identify the differences between the two images.
9. Modify the prompt based on the differences and generate another version.
10. Continue refining the prompt until a visually similar result is obtained.

# EXAMPLE 1 – MINIMALIST JAPANESE TEA ROOM

## Analysis of the Target Image

The first reference image shows a calm and simple Japanese tea room. It contains natural wooden surfaces, a traditional shoji screen, a tatami floor, a small bonsai tree, and a ceramic tea set. Soft daylight enters the room through the screen, giving the scene a peaceful and balanced appearance.

### Key Features

* **Objects:** Bonsai tree, ceramic tea set, tatami flooring, and shoji screen.
* **Colors:** Cream, light brown, white, and small amounts of green.
* **Lighting:** Soft and diffused natural daylight.
* **Textures:** Woven tatami, smooth wooden surfaces, paper screen, and ceramic.
* **Composition:** Spacious and symmetrical room viewed from a low angle.
* **Style:** Minimalist Japanese interior with a photorealistic CGI appearance.

## Prompts Used – Example 1

### Basic Prompt

```text
A simple Japanese tea room with a bonsai tree.
```

### Refined Prompt

```text
A minimalist Japanese tea room with light wooden walls,
traditional tatami flooring, a small bonsai tree and a
ceramic tea set, illuminated by soft natural daylight.
```

### Final Prompt

```text
Photorealistic minimalist Japanese tea room with traditional
tatami flooring, light pine wood walls, a large shoji screen
with soft diffused daylight, one small bonsai tree and a
handcrafted ceramic tea set, clean symmetrical arrangement,
low floor-level camera perspective, wabi-sabi style, peaceful
atmosphere, realistic textures, detailed architectural CGI,
minimal furniture and no unnecessary decorations.
```

## Comparison Report – Example 1

The generated image successfully reproduced the main elements of the reference, including the Japanese tea room, bonsai tree, and tea set. The natural wooden and cream color scheme was also represented well. The soft lighting helped create a similar peaceful atmosphere. The overall minimalist composition was maintained, although the exact size, shape, and position of some objects could differ slightly.

**Overall:** The final prompt produced a closer result because it contained specific information about the room, materials, lighting, composition, and visual style.

# EXAMPLE 2 – FUTURISTIC CITY AT NIGHT

## Analysis of the Target Image

The second reference image presents a futuristic city during nighttime. It contains tall glass skyscrapers, bright digital advertisements, flying vehicles, and wet roads reflecting colorful lights. The combination of neon lighting, mist, reflections, and modern buildings gives the scene a cinematic science-fiction appearance.

### Key Features

* **Objects:** Skyscrapers, flying vehicles, roads, and digital billboards.
* **Colors:** Blue, cyan, purple, and pink neon shades.
* **Lighting:** Bright artificial neon lights with reflections on wet surfaces.
* **Textures:** Glass buildings, metallic vehicles, and wet roads.
* **Composition:** Wide-angle view with tall buildings extending into the distance.
* **Style:** Cinematic, futuristic, and photorealistic.

## Prompts Used – Example 2

### Basic Prompt

```text
A futuristic city at night with tall buildings and neon lights.
```

```text
A futuristic city at night with tall glass skyscrapers,
bright neon advertisements, flying vehicles and wet roads
reflecting blue and purple lights.
```

```text
Cinematic photorealistic futuristic city at night, huge glass
skyscrapers covered with glowing holographic billboards,
flying vehicles moving between buildings, wet reflective
streets, blue, cyan, purple and pink neon illumination, light
mist in the atmosphere, detailed metallic surfaces,
```

```text
      ↓

```

# OBSERVATIONS

1. A short and simple prompt usually produces a general interpretation of the reference image.
2. Mentioning the important objects makes the generated image more accurate.
3. Describing colors and lighting helps the AI reproduce the overall visual appearance.
4. Adding style keywords helps achieve the required artistic or realistic look.
5. Camera position and composition have an important effect on the arrangement of objects.
6. Repeatedly refining the prompt can improve the similarity between the generated and reference images.

# CONCLUSION

This experiment demonstrated how text-to-image AI tools can be used to recreate the visual appearance of a reference image through prompt engineering. By carefully describing objects, colors, lighting, textures, composition, perspective, and style, the basic prompt could be gradually improved. The experiment showed that detailed and well-organized prompts generally provide results that are more similar to the target image.

# RESULT

The selected reference images were successfully reproduced using progressively refined prompts. The generated images were compared with the original images, and the prompts were modified to improve their visual similarity. This demonstrated the importance of detailed prompt writing in AI-based image generation.
